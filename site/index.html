Your reading goes where you go.

Everything lives in one folder on your computer, called your Library: the sites you follow, every article, what you’ve read and starred. Copy it to a USB stick and your reading travels with you.

On Windows and Linux, Feedrico can even run straight from the stick, so nothing has to be installed on the other computer.

  1. At home

    All the sites you follow, a few thousand articles and that one essay you starred, in a single folder.

  2. In a pocket

    Copy the folder to a USB stick, like you would copy holiday photos. That’s all there is to it.

  3. On the train

    No signal, and nothing missing. Articles and pictures were saved the last time you were online.

  4. At a friend’s

    Plug the stick in and open Feedrico. Your friend’s computer stays exactly as it was.

  5. As you left it

    The same article, still starred, right where you stopped. Your settings come along too.

What you read is your business.

Feedrico has no account, no ads and no tracking. It connects to the internet for two things only.

The sites you follow
To fetch their new articles and pictures, every half hour while Feedrico is open or whenever you ask.
Updates
Only when you click Check for updates. Feedrico never updates itself behind your back.

That’s all.

Reading an article sends nothing anywhere, so no website learns what you opened or how long you stayed. We don’t collect statistics either: we couldn’t tell you how many people use Feedrico if we wanted to.

Feedrico’s code is public, so anyone can check that this is true.

Good questions.

What does Feedrico do?
It collects new articles from the websites, blogs and newsletters you choose and shows them in one tidy list, newest first. Apps like this are called RSS readers or feed readers. You pick the sites; nothing else gets in.
Is it really free?
Yes. Feedrico is free to download and use on Windows, Mac and Linux. There are no ads, no subscription and no paid version.
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for. Download Feedrico, add a site you like, and start reading.
How do I add a site?
Paste the address of a website you like, and Feedrico finds its articles. Coming from another reader? Import the file it exports (an OPML file) and all your sites move over at once.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Feedrico saves articles and their pictures whenever it is online, so you can keep reading on a train or a plane.
Can I use it on more than one computer?
Yes. Everything is kept in one folder called your Library. Copy that folder to a USB stick or another computer and your reading comes with you. Feedrico does not sync by itself, because it has no servers.
Does Feedrico track what I read?
No. Feedrico connects only to the sites you follow to fetch new articles, and to GitHub when you ask it to check for updates. Nobody, including us, can see what you read.
I read a lot. Is it fast?
Yes. Twenty sites or two thousand, the list stays smooth and search covers every article. There are keyboard shortcuts for everything, if you like those.
